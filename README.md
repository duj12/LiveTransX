# LiveTransX 🎙️  →  🌐

**On-Device Real-Time Voice Translation**

LiveTransX translates your voice in real time — entirely on your device. No internet required. No audio uploaded. Your conversations stay 100% private.

**🌐 Website:** https://duj12.github.io/LiveTransX/

---

## ✨ Features

### Free
- 🎤 **Microphone Translation** — Speak and see live bilingual subtitles instantly
- 🧠 **Apple Speech Analyzer** — Powered by iOS 26 on-device speech recognition
- 🌍 **Apple Translation** — 30+ languages, automatic source detection
- 📱 **iPhone & iPad** — Native support for both

## ✨ Features

### Free
- 🎤 **Microphone Translation** — Speak and see live bilingual subtitles instantly
- 🧠 **Apple Speech Analyzer** — Powered by iOS 26 on-device speech recognition
- 🌍 **Apple Translation** — 35+ languages, automatic source detection
- 📱 **iPhone & iPad** — Native support for both

### Pro (Monthly Subscription)
- 🪟 **Floating Translation Window** — Picture-in-Picture overlay alongside any app
- 📺 **Screen Share Translation** — Translate audio from video calls, meetings, and videos
- 📁 **Audio File Translation** — Transcribe and translate mp3/wav files
- 📝 **SRT Subtitle Export** — Export bilingual, original-only, or translation-only subtitles

### Supported Languages
35 source languages and 28 target languages:
English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Cantonese, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Hindi, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Czech, Greek, Hungarian, Romanian, Persian, Catalan, Filipino, Hebrew, Croatian, Macedonian, Norwegian, Slovak, Ukrainian.

## 🔒 Privacy First

- ✅ **100% on-device processing** — Audio never leaves your device
- ✅ **No account required** — No sign-up, no login
- ✅ **No tracking** — Zero analytics SDKs, zero ads
- ✅ **No data collection** — Your conversations are yours alone
