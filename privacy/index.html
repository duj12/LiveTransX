LiveTransX — Real-time Voice Translation That Never Leaves Your Phone

Real-time on-device speech translation with floating window, screen share, and file transcription. No internet needed.

Download on the App Store

Translate conversations instantly, with zero cloud dependency

LiveTransX is a fully on-device real-time speech translation app for iPhone and Mac. Every word of speech recognition and translation is processed locally on your device — there is no server to upload to, no audio ever leaves your hardware, and the app works completely offline, even on a flight. Your conversations stay 100% private by design: no account to create, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no ads.

Built on Apple's on-device AI (iOS 26 SpeechAnalyzer and the Apple Translation framework) and backed by five speech-recognition engines, LiveTransX automatically picks the most accurate engine for the language it hears. It detects the source language automatically, shows live bilingual subtitles as you speak, and supports 42 source languages and 36 target languages — with the interface itself available in 29 languages.

Why on-device translation matters

Cloud-based translators send your voice to a server and back. That means a network dependency, a privacy trade-off, and noticeable latency. LiveTransX removes all three: recognition runs locally, translation runs locally, and the floating subtitles update in real time without ever waiting on a connection. For international meetings, cross-border video calls, foreign films, travel, or simply learning a new language, you get instant, private, offline translation.
